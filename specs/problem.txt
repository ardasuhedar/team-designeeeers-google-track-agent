Task Summary:
- Verify a synchronous up/down counter with registered output `value` and combinational output `value_next`.

Clocking and Control:
- State updates happen on the rising edge of `clk`.
- `rst` is synchronous and active high. When asserted, `value` must load `initial_value`.
- `reinit` is also synchronous and active high. When asserted, it loads `initial_value` and overrides any concurrent increment or decrement requests.

Behavior To Verify:
- If only `incr_valid` is high, the counter increases by `incr`.
- If only `decr_valid` is high, the counter decreases by `decr`.
- If both `incr_valid` and `decr_valid` are high in the same cycle, the net change `incr - decr` is applied.
- If neither valid signal is high, the counter holds its current value.
- `incr` and `decr` are each in the range 0 to 3.

Range and Wrap-Around:
- The counter range is inclusive 0 through 10.
- Overflow wraps around within that range.
- Underflow also wraps around within that range.
- Directed tests should cover boundary cases such as incrementing near 10 and decrementing near 0.

Output Semantics:
- `value` is the registered state and reflects the previous cycle's chosen operation after the next rising edge.
- `value_next` is combinational and should show the next counter state implied by the current `value`, `reinit`, `incr_valid`, `decr_valid`, `incr`, and `decr` inputs.

Verification Objective:
- Generate a self-checking Verilog testbench that checks reset, reinitialization priority, hold behavior, increment, decrement, simultaneous increment and decrement, wrap-around, and the relationship between `value` and `value_next`.
- The testbench should pass the correct RTL and fail incorrect RTL candidates whenever observable behavior allows it.
